# DIU CPC 99 Days — FAQ

Q: Is this only for expert programmers?
A: No. The journey begins with foundations and scales upward.

Q: Can I choose one track?
A: Yes. Students may choose a primary interest while still following the shared 99-day structure.

Q: How much time should I spend daily?
A: Usually 45–120 minutes. Project days may require more.

Q: Can I use AI?
A: Yes, as a learning assistant. Students should understand and be able to explain their submitted work.

Q: Does the GPT track my GitHub automatically?
A: Not unless an authenticated integration is connected. Without one, it can only review information the student provides.

Q: Does finishing the GPT conversation guarantee a certificate?
A: No. Official eligibility is determined by DIU CPC.

Q: What if I miss a day?
A: Resume rather than quit. The goal is sustainable progress, not perfection.

Q: Can I switch tracks?
A: Yes. The mentor can adapt the roadmap.

Q: Can I build a real project?
A: Yes. The final phase is designed around project planning, development, testing, documentation, and demo preparation.
